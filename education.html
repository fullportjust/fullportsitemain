Non-negotiable

Sizing keeps you here. Not entries.

People don’t blow up because they were wrong. They blow up because they were too big. Options can expire worthless.

Position sizing

  • FULL3–5% of your account. The default.
  • SMALL1–3%. When you’re less sure.
  • LOTTOUnder 1%. High risk, and it can go to zero.

Risk by time left

DTE means days to expiration — how long the contract has left.

  • 1–7 daysMost dangerous. Loses value fastest.
  • 8–14 daysStill risky.
  • 15–30 daysMore forgiving.
  • 30+ daysLowest risk of the four. More time to be right.
