Questions

Before you join.

Is the free Discord really free? +
Yes. Free forever, no card, no trial period and no expiry. You join at no cost and you are never charged.
What do I get when I join? +
Live stock alerts, the full education, our indicator setups and the shared watchlist — plus a room you can post in and talk to us directly.
Do I need money to start? +
Not to join, not to learn, and not to follow along. To place trades yourself you’d need a brokerage account (Webull, Robinhood or similar) and your own capital — we generally suggest around $500 to trade comfortably. Trading always carries risk of loss, so never use money you can’t afford to lose.
Do I need any experience? +
None. Most people here started without ever placing a trade. The education starts at reading a chart, and beginner questions are the normal kind in this room.
